A cozy-chaotic roguelite for iPhone. Free with optional ads. Unbox a plush figure, then brawl through three floors of a toy shop after dark: grumpy jack-in-the-boxes, rubber ducks, wind-up soldiers and three bosses. Every run you pick new gifts to build your toy's powers.
