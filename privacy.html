Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Shelf Brawlers is a single-player game made by Ruslan Akhmetvaleev ("we"). We have no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

Your game progress. Coins, unlocked figures, stickers and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example a revive or double Coins) and occasional ads between runs. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), your approximate location from your IP address, and information about ads you see and tap, plus crash and performance data. How Google uses this is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can refuse tracking in the prompt, or later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; you will then see non-personalised ads. In the EU and UK the game asks for your consent with Google's form, and you can change it any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options.

Purchases. If the game sells in-app items, Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes. We will update this page and the App Store privacy details before a version that changes how data is handled is released.

Contact. Questions: support@licman.dev
